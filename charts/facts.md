## Chart notes (footnotes for the post; not on the images)

- 1_ice_new_hires_who_left: People hired since September 2025 with no prior federal civilian or military service: running total each month, by whether they are still there. New to federal service: under 3 months of creditable federal civilian or military service at hire. ICE officers: series 1801 and 1811; Border Patrol agents: series 1896; both on the GL law-enforcement pay plan. OPM's public files don't follow individual people, so a departure's hire month is its effective month minus its years of service; in every month the running count who have left is at most the running count hired (checked). Transfers to other agencies are not counted. Recent months fill in as late records arrive. OPM's "other separation" category includes removals for discipline or performance, and deaths. Source: OPM Enterprise Human Resources Integration (EHRI) data, files through August 2026 (entry law-enforcement pay plan). Permanent appointments.
- 2_how_people_left: Permanent staff who left in 2025, as a share of January 2025 staff, by how they left. Layoff (RIF): separations agencies coded as a reduction in force. Other: terminations (expired appointments and other) and OPM's "other separation", which includes removals for discipline or performance, and deaths. Retirement includes disability retirement. Transfers to other agencies are not counted. Source: OPM Enterprise Human Resources Integration (EHRI) data, files through August 2026. Permanent appointments.
- 3_who_left_by_service: Permanent federal employees who left each year, by years of service. *2026 is January through August only, so its bar is not comparable in height with the full years. DRP exits are included; transfers to other agencies are not. Source: OPM Enterprise Human Resources Integration (EHRI) data, files through August 2026. Permanent appointments.
- 4_leaving_pace_by_service: Permanent employees who left per month, per 1,000 on staff in January. January through August of every year. Each row is shaded against its own range. All departures include DRP exits; quits and retirements don't. Transfers to other agencies are not counted. Retirement includes disability retirement. Records for recent months are still arriving: once they do, 2026* figures are likely 2-19% higher. Source: OPM Enterprise Human Resources Integration (EHRI) data, files through August 2026. Permanent appointments.
- 6_attorney_quits: Permanent employees who quit per month, per 1,000 on staff in January. January through August of every year. Each row is shaded against its own range. DRP exits are not counted as quits. Attorneys: occupational series 0905 (general attorney); the agencies shown are those with 300+ attorneys whose attorneys are quitting at 2+ times their 2015-2024 high. Air traffic controllers: series 2152, every agency. Graduate degree: master's, professional or doctorate. Records for recent months are still arriving: once they do, 2026* quits are likely 2-10% higher. Source: OPM Enterprise Human Resources Integration (EHRI) data, files through August 2026. Permanent appointments.
- 8_pathways_hires: New hires through Pathways (internships, Recent Graduates, Presidential Management Fellows), January-August of each year, all appointment types. Pathways: OPM's pathways_group field on the hire record. *2026 is January through August; every year here covers the same months. Source: OPM Enterprise Human Resources Integration (EHRI) data, files through August 2026. All appointment types.

Source: OPM Enterprise Human Resources Integration (EHRI) data, files through August 2026. All appointment types.

Source: OPM Enterprise Human Resources Integration (EHRI) data, files through August 2026. All appointment types.
